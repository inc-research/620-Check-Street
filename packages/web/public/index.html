Third Floor

How a fund that uses options actually responded to the market, on the days it rose and the days it fell.

Third Floor describes how a fixed list of funds that use options responded to the market over past months. It does not give advice, rank funds, or say what to hold, and it says nothing about what any of them does next. Nothing about you is uploaded or stored, and the only files this page fetches are the published reports on this site.

A companion to First Floor and Second Floor. Code under MIT; the written explanations under CC BY 4.0.
